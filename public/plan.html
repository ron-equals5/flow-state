01

The approach

An experienced developer working with Claude builds the Flow State platform: the website, booking, memberships, waivers, front desk, instructor tools, CRM journeys, reporting and the app. We rent only what's regulated and commodity.

Why custom
  • The custom parts are the brand. The Flow, the Reset Room and Restricted Flow blocks are what make Flow State different.
  • Arketa's API is too limited to build these on: no membership sales, class creation, room booking, waitlists or change notifications.
  • Own the data and experience, with no vendor limits or tier upsells.
  • Lower running cost: about $200–450 a month vs. $650–1,150 on Arketa.
Options considered
  • Custom-first: chosen. Full control, lowest running cost.
  • Hybrid on Arketa: fallback only, if the go/no-go fails. Arketa runs month to month.
  • Off-the-shelf only: rejected. The Flow and Reset Room would be limited to what the tools already do.
Rule of thumb: build what makes Flow State different; rent what's regulated.

06

The core design

Bookings don't subtract from a simple credit count. Every purchase creates entitlements, every booking is checked against eligibility rules, and every change is written to an append-only ledger. Getting this right early keeps every later feature cheap.

Unlimited membership

Valid for the billing period, with optional limits such as one booking per day.

Finite monthly membership

N credits per period that reset at renewal.

Packs & single classes

N credits with an expiration date.

Events

Member credits don't apply; needs an event ticket.

Series

One purchase reserves every class in the series.

Reset Room

The booker's entitlement covers the slot; a guest pass or the invitee's own entitlement covers the guest.

Restricted Flow blocks

Only eligible products or groups can book.

Late cancel & no-show

Rules forfeit the credit or charge a fee.

Open questions

For the studio

  1. Opening date. What's the exact date, and when must booking go live? This sets the go/no-go.
  2. The Flow. Which hours run as Flow blocks, and how many people per start time?
  3. Restricted Flow blocks. Who are they restricted to, and why?
  4. Reset Room. Slot length and price? Do credits apply? How do guest passes work?
  5. Pricing. The full list, plus late-cancel and no-show rules, and the Founding Membership offer.
  6. On-demand video. Needed at launch or later? Where will the content come from?
  7. Hardware. Which readers and printer? Is door access in scope?
  8. Staff chat. Slack or WhatsApp?
  9. Content. Who supplies class descriptions, instructor bios and photos, and when?

For the build

  1. Support. Who handles bugs and front-desk issues day to day, and after hours?
  2. ClassPass. Are aggregators wanted? Integrating needs their partner approval.
  3. Instructor pay. Per class, per head or tiered? It shapes the payroll export.
12

Risks

Ownership

We are the vendor

Support, uptime and one person holding the knowledge. Runbooks, tests, a CLAUDE.md and docs so another developer (or Claude) can pick it up, plus monitoring with alerts.

Timeline

Opening is this month

Opening-ready scope only, go/no-go one week out, and a manual or Arketa month-to-month fallback.

Money

Payment edge cases

Failed payments, freezes, prorating, refunds, late-cancel fees. Stripe charges; the ledger tracks credits; webhooks are idempotent; every rule has tests; nightly reconciliation.

Approvals

Twilio, Apple, Stripe

Outside approvals take weeks. They start this week.

Reliability

Front desk at 6am

Durable Objects for capacity, a printable roster fallback, monitoring and D1 point-in-time restore.

Security

Client data & waivers

Least-privilege roles, Cloudflare Access on staff tools, signed waivers in private R2 storage.
